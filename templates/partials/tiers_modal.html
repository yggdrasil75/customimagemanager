🗄 Storage Tiers

Order tiers fastest → slowest. Files stay at their normal library paths as symlinks; bytes live on the tier drives. Keep the media/ folder itself (links, DB, thumbnails) on your fastest drive. Ratios are normalised, so 5 / 50 / 45 works as percentages.

📦 Small-file packing

Folds images and their sidecars (everything except video) into large .packs/ archives to cut inode count and open file descriptors. The packs are the source of truth; the database is only a rebuildable cache. Videos and tiered (symlinked) files are left alone.

Pack size and max-open-packs apply the next time the app starts; the auto-migrate and idle settings take effect immediately.
