Barcode model

The barcode model localises codes so each one can be cropped and decoded on its own. Without it, a scan can only read codes already large and clean enough to be found in the full frame, so small or angled ones are missed. Any YOLO detect model whose classes are named barcode, qr, ean and the like will work — including one from Quick Train on your own boxes. Codes that are detected but can't be decoded are still marked, so a blurred barcode leaves a record of where it is.

Larger face models catch small and profile faces that n misses — those extra detections are what let a cluster hold together. Changing the size needs a Rescan all to pick them up. Ignored when a Face model is chosen explicitly. (yolov11-face ships n/s/m/l; there is no x.)
