Build shipped duplicate detectors

Images under these folders are turned into synthetic pairs — re-encoded, resized, nudged, slightly cropped (duplicates); boxed over, cropped hard, paired with unrelated images (not duplicates) — and the logistic heuristic + siamese CNN are fitted on them. Every epoch draws fresh pairs. A held-out slice of images scores the result per pair kind. Output goes to the modules' pretrained/ folders, ready to commit.
